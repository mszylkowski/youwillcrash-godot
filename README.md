You Will Crash
</h1>

You Will Crash is a casual game about avoiding obstacles that come your way while you climb the levels and encounter increasingly difficult patterns. It was re-written from scratch in Godot after an initial launch in Lua+Love2D. It's available for free without ads on Android via the Play Store.

<p align="center">
<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.montevisoft.youwillcrash"><img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/7/78/Google_Play_Store_badge_EN.svg"></a>
</p>

## Store page

**Dodge hordes of dynamic obstacles in this fast-paced, addictive arcade game!** Swipe to survive, collect points to unlock new modes, and challenge your friends to get the highest score. Master unpredictable threats in this skill-based, ad-free experience!
